# Product and print specifications

Use these as optional sourcing leads and illustrative starting specifications, not selected products, current stock, price guarantees, or specifications for every advisor. Recheck live official pages and the exact chosen product before final export. The selected supplier's verified template controls artwork; supplier changes require their own templates.

Local availability, delivery country, budget, and timing may change the selection.

## Mailing envelope and shipping carrier

The custom envelope is purchased from a packaging printer and shipped through the selected carrier. FedEx is the starting example; follow another carrier's actual packaging and label requirements when the advisor selects it. Distinguish the branded artwork from the recipient shipping label. Do not describe it as a carrier-manufactured custom envelope or add carrier trade dress as the advisor's branding.

A product-specific example for a nominal 10.5 × 15-inch mailer used an **8 × 14-inch printable area**, displayed on an **11 × 17-inch template sheet** with flap and guides. Landscape art used **14 × 8 inches** within that area, and that template prohibited printing on the flap and black backgrounds. These are example constraints, not a current template. Obtain the selected product's actual template rather than applying a generic full-bleed canvas to the nominal envelope size.

Reserve an uncluttered **6 × 4-inch area** when using an actual **4 × 6-inch adhesive shipping label without a document tab**. Verify the selected service/printer's label format. Larger labels or pouches require a different reserve. A placeholder may appear in a review PDF; remove placeholder borders, text, sample addresses and barcodes from branding artwork.

Keep the return address and brand content outside the label reserve. Have prepress confirm orientation and printable margins. Inspect usable internal dimensions after padding/seams and fit one assembled packet including the secured pen. Nominal outside dimensions alone do not establish fit.

For FedEx, consult [its shipping label guidance](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/create-shipping-label.html) and the selected service's current packaging requirements. Do not promise One Rate eligibility for custom packaging. For another carrier, consult its official equivalent. Record outgoing postage separately using actual packed weight, dimensions, origin and destination. Digital design cannot verify a shipped sample's condition.

## Notepad

- Trim 5.5 × 8.5 inches; with 0.125-inch bleed on all sides, canvas 5.75 × 8.75.
- One artwork page is the repeated sheet design. Fifty pads of 25 sheets means 1,250 sheets, not 50 loose sheets.
- A product-specific UPrinting example required important content at least 0.5 inch from the glued edge. Verify glue clearance, safe margins, and export details using the selected pad's own template/prepress; a same-size flyer template does not prove them.
- White uncoated paper supports handwriting. Keep rules faint, body area open, branding compact and text clear of the binding.

## Top-fold handwritten card

- Closed size 5.5 × 4.25 inches; unfolded 5.5 × 8.5. Bleed canvas 5.75 × 8.75 when the chosen vendor specifies 0.125-inch bleed.
- For the starting landscape top-fold layout, the exterior front is the lower half and the exterior back is rotated 180 degrees in the upper half. The inside is blank. Confirm that imposition against the selected printer's fold/orientation template before export.
- Confirm scoring/folding, writable uncoated stock, exterior-only print handling and whether plain envelopes are included. A plain included envelope does not include custom branding.
- [PrintingCenterUSA card templates](https://www.printingcenterusa.com/templates/note-card) may contain surrounding legends/marks beyond the required art canvas. Do not mistake a whole template page for the finished or bleed size.
- Use the selected printer's current file preparation guidance, such as [PrintingCenterUSA's](https://www.printingcenterusa.com/help-center/file-preparation/). A vendor-supplied ICC profile or PDF preset applies only when that printer confirms it for the chosen product; do not impose one printer's profile on all pieces.

## Branded A2 envelope

- A2 closed size 5.75 × 4.375 inches fits the initial 5.5 × 4.25-inch card. Verify actual stock and flap geometry.
- Front: small authentic logo in the permitted upper-left print area; center clear for handwritten family name. Back: restrained brand-color accent on the flap when supported.
- The closed envelope rectangle is a design reference, not a manufacturing dieline. Obtain the printer's margins, seam/flap template, front/back capabilities and quantity requirements before preparing final print files.
- Construction seam lines shown in a review drawing must not print. Do not send a seam illustration as back artwork.
- If rear printing is unavailable, present a front-only or branded-seal alternative for review; do not silently drop the approved flap treatment.

## Pen

- Select the product, color and decoration first; then fit the authentic logo/contact detail to its documented imprint area. Do not transfer another model's imprint dimensions.
- Obtain minimum type size, line/stroke width, color workflow, supported file format and exact template. Use vector logo artwork; outline text in the production export if required while preserving an editable source.
- Stock color names and generated mockups cannot establish color matching. Prefer a neutral barrel and full-color printing when no stock barrel matches the brand. Engraving removes the opportunity to reproduce multicolor branding.
- Do not simplify an approved full logo to an icon or remove the contact line just to fit without showing the change. Choose a compatible product or obtain user agreement to the revised design.
- Confirm the cost for the actual decoration and quantity, not the site's default price. Include setup and proof/sample fees if quoted. Printed color and tiny-letter legibility need an actual supplier proof/sample.

## Four-panel brochure

- Trim 11 × 8.5 inches flat; two equal panels; 5.5 × 8.5 closed. For 0.125-inch outer bleed: **11.25 × 8.75-inch two-page PDF**.
- Outside spread: back on left, front on right. Inside spread: inside left, inside right. Supply separate four-page reading PDF for advisor viewing.
- Extend backgrounds into the verified bleed; keep live text clear of trim and fold. Preserve generous editorial margins even when a template permits tighter safe guides, and verify the selected printer's current template.
- Use native type/vector logos; target suitable effective image resolution at placed size, normally around 300 ppi when requested by the printer. Do not upscale/AI-reinvent a real person to disguise insufficient resolution.
- Approximate content: cover 20–50 words, team/value 80–120, services 100–150, first appointment/contact 60–100. Treat these as editing aids, not quotas. Allow room for actual required firm text.
- These are marketing materials, not a substitute for any required regulatory brochure. Use supplied firm wording and flag missing required footer text; do not create generic legal filler.

## Office directions sheet

Follow [Office directions](office-directions.md) for the template, map, QR and alignment checks. Supply an editable Word file and one-page PDF. On ordinary office printers, use Fit to printable area if the design reaches the edges; expect a white border. Commercial edge-to-edge printing requires the selected printer’s bleed specification and a prepared export. Do not call the Letter-size review PDF a bleed-ready commercial file. Record the chosen printing route and quantity; do not invent a vendor quote.

## Product evidence record

For each chosen item record: exact product name/URL, checked date, needed quantity, planned order quantity/minimum, size, stock/material, colors, sides, finishing, template URL/local file, imprint/bleed/safe geometry, file/color requirements, quoted price/configuration and production/transit timing. Label unavailable fields precisely. A product page option is not a configured quote, and a downloaded template is not proof acceptance.
