# Final advisor handoff

Read when all requested edits are complete and the user has requested the handoff, including a request made at the start. The recipient is the advisor who needs to see the project and order the pieces. Deliver a coherent folder and ZIP, not a dump of development files.

Names may change for clarity, but the guide must reference the actual relative paths. Include only the selected versions. Store editable design sources and build scripts in the project workspace; include them in the delivery only if useful to the advisor or printer. Do not include temporary PDFs, rendered Word QA pages, sample prospect addresses, private research files or superseded artwork.

The combined project preview should show all seven items and identify their dimensions/use. Make individual designs readable in their own PDFs. Include both front and back of the inner envelope and the card's intentionally blank inside. The pen's reference PDF must say when it is not an exact-size imprint file.

Mockups show appearance. They do not establish printable artwork, stock color, physical fit, fold geometry, or carrier certification. Use separate clean, correctly sized artwork in each printer folder. Label supplier templates as references and keep their guides out of printable art.

For the office directions sheet, include its readable page in the preview, the fixed-layout PDF, editable Word source and printing notes. A photographic mockup is optional for this flat insert. Identify office printing or the actual copy service, one sheet per in-person packet (50 by default), stock, scaling/border expectations and any commercial bleed preparation. Include the sheet with the brochure during assembly. Verify the rendered QR points to the current advisor’s actual meeting address; test a physical copy before the full run.

## Word ordering guide

Use available document authoring tools; when a documents skill is available, follow its authoring/render workflow. Make an editable `.docx`, not renamed text or a PDF substitute. Render it and inspect all pages before delivery. If creation or rendering is unavailable, provide the available content and identify the guide or inspection as unfinished. Keep its language direct, addressed to the advisor, and organized by product.

Include:

1. **Purpose and shopping list:** one of each of the seven items per recipient; target packet count; recommended supplier and order quantity per item; minimum-order surplus.
2. **Exact product links:** clickable official product pages, product names/model identifiers where available, and the verification date. Say when links are generic product pages rather than saved configurations.
3. **Options to select:** dimensions, stock/material, finish, sides, colors, sheets per pad, fold/glue edge, writable areas, and the specific pen decoration/barrel choice. Distinguish confirmed options from a custom quote request.
4. **Files to send:** exact relative folder/file names, what each file is for, and any templates/brand masters needed. Clearly distinguish reader PDFs and mockups from printable artwork.
5. **Production readiness:** what the printer must confirm, adapt or convert. If exact pen/envelope geometry is unresolved, state that before checkout and include a precise request for the supplier. Do not hide missing production artwork behind “final.”
6. **Costs and timing:** verified configured prices only, with date/quantity/configuration and exclusions. Otherwise say “quote required”; do not substitute default website prices. Include setup, proofs, tax, inbound delivery and separate outgoing postage for the selected carrier. Distinguish production days from transit and ask for an in-hand estimate tied to the actual delivery postal code/date.
7. **Proof and order steps:** confirm real firm/contact/footer details, check supplier proofs, review printed brand color where needed, and verify one complete packed sample. Design approval and supplier proof approval are separate facts.
8. **Assembly:** one of each item, personalized handwritten note, secured pen, fit/protection check, accurate parcel weight/dimensions and real recipient-specific carrier label. Include a short adaptable note with clearly marked personalization fields.

Link sources beside the product information they support. Do not reproduce long product descriptions, include fabricated estimates, or copy another advisor's language. If a supplier's prepress service is needed, request it explicitly rather than assuming it is included or free.

## Finish available production work

Before handing off, do all local export work supported by the verified templates: outer bleed, correct folds and page order, clean print art, font embedding/outlining where required, image checks and the chosen color workflow. Keep editable masters. Do not simply leave known bleed/color fixes to the advisor when they can be completed correctly in the session.

If a vendor-specific specification or advisor-supplied footer is still missing, preserve the selected design and identify the item as **requiring prepress preparation** with the exact next action. The advisor can request that service using the guide. The overall handoff can still be delivered at the user's request, but do not call all files printer-ready or promise one-click ordering.

## Final checks and delivery

- Open every included PDF. Verify pages, trim/bleed dimensions, blank intentional pages, layout and copy. Confirm all seven items are represented and no previous advisor identifiers remain.
- Check the guide against actual files and product configurations. Verify its hyperlinks and inspect its rendered pages for clipping, awkward breaks and unreadable tables.
- Refresh mockups after the last relevant design changes. Compare them to the PDFs; do not leave a rejected color or old contact detail in the final picture set.
- Ensure the ZIP opens, paths are portable, and all named assets are present. Keep the final folder and ZIP together under a clear deliverables location.
- Summarize what is included and any material remaining production actions. Link the ZIP, project preview and Word guide. No vendor orders or messages are implied by delivery.
